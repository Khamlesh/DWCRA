## System Overview

The DWCRA (Development of Women and Children in Rural Areas) System is a comprehensive web-based platform designed to manage self-help groups, loans, and payments. The system supports three main user roles:

- **Group Leaders**: Manage groups and apply for loans
- **Group Members**: Make payments and track payment history
- **Administrators**: Oversee the entire system, manage users, and approve loans

---

## Accessing the System

### Step 1: Open Your Web Browser
1. Launch your preferred web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
2. Navigate to the DWCRA system URL provided by your organization
3. The system will display the login page

### Step 2: Language Selection (Optional)
- The system supports multiple languages (English and Telugu)
- Click on the language toggle button to switch between languages
- Your language preference will be saved for future sessions

---

## New User Registration

### Step 1: Access Registration Page
1. On the login page, click the **"New User? Register Here"** link
2. You will be redirected to the registration form

### Step 2: Fill Registration Form
Complete all required fields:

1. **Unique ID**: Enter your assigned unique identification number
2. **Full Name**: Enter your complete name as per official documents
3. **Email Address**: Provide a valid email address for notifications
4. **Bank Name**: Enter the name of your bank branch
5. **Password**: Create a strong password (minimum 8 characters)
6. **Role Selection**: Choose your role:
   - **Group Leader**: If you will be leading a self-help group
   - **Group Member**: If you will be a member of a group

### Step 3: Submit Registration
1. Review all entered information for accuracy
2. Click the **"Register"** button
3. If successful, you'll see a confirmation message
4. Click **"Already have an account? Login here"** to proceed to login

### Step 4: Identity Verification (Required)
After registration, you must verify your identity:
1. Log in to your account
2. Navigate to your **Profile** page
3. Upload required identity documents
4. Wait for administrator approval

## User Login Process

### Step 1: Enter Credentials
1. On the login page, enter your **Unique ID**
2. Enter your **Password**
3. Ensure both fields are filled correctly

### Step 2: Submit Login
1. Click the **"Secure Login"** button
2. The system will validate your credentials
3. If successful, you'll be redirected to your role-specific dashboard

### Step 3: Password Recovery (If Needed)
If you forget your password:
1. Click **"Forgot Password?"** on the login page
2. Enter your email address
3. Check your email for password reset instructions
4. Follow the link to create a new password

## Group Leader Workflow

### Dashboard Overview
After login, group leaders see:
- Welcome message with their name
- Quick action buttons
- Group and loan status information
- Loan repayment progress

### Step 1: Identity Verification (First Time)
Before creating groups, verify your identity:
1. Click **"Profile"** button
2. Upload required identity documents
3. Submit for administrator approval
4. Wait for approval notification

### Step 2: Create Group
1. Click **"Create Group"** button
2. Enter unique IDs for 6 group members
3. Ensure all members:
   - Are registered in the system
   - Have verified their identity
   - Belong to the same bank as the leader
4. Click **"Create Group"** to submit

### Step 3: Apply for Loan
1. Click **"Apply for Loan"** button
2. Fill in loan application details:
   - Loan amount
   - Purpose of loan
   - Repayment period
   - Supporting documents
3. Submit application
4. Wait for administrator approval

### Step 4: Monitor Group Status
- View group member information
- Track loan application status
- Monitor repayment progress
- Access payment history

### Step 5: Group Management
- View all group members
- Check member payment status
- Access group reports
- Manage group activities

- Group management features

1. Click **"Users"** in the navigation menu

   - **Create New User**: Add users manually
   - **Edit User**: Modify user information

2. Review pending verification requests

4. Send notifications to users

2. Generate various reports:
   - User activity reports

3. Export reports in different formats

### Step 6: Notifications
1. Click **"Notifications"** in the navigation
2. Send system-wide notifications

### Step 7: System Settings

## Logout Process

1. Click the **"Logout"** button from your dashboard

## Troubleshooting

### Common Login Issues

**Problem**: "Invalid credentials" error
**Solution**:
1. Check that Unique ID is entered correctly
2. Ensure password is correct (case-sensitive)
3. Try resetting password if forgotten

**Problem**: Account locked
**Solution**:
1. Contact administrator to unlock account
2. Verify identity if required
3. Reset password if necessary

### Registration Issues

**Problem**: "Unique ID already exists" error
**Solution**:
1. Contact administrator to verify your Unique ID
2. Use a different email address if needed
3. Ensure you're not already registered

**Problem**: Registration form not submitting
**Solution**:
1. Check that all required fields are filled
2. Ensure email format is correct
3. Verify password meets requirements

### Payment Issues

**Problem**: Payment not processing
**Solution**:
1. Check internet connection
2. Verify payment details are correct
3. Contact administrator if issue persists

**Problem**: Payment history not showing
**Solution**:
1. Refresh the page
2. Check date range filters
3. Contact administrator if data is missing

### System Access Issues

**Problem**: Cannot access certain features
**Solution**:
1. Verify your user role has required permissions
2. Complete identity verification if pending
3. Contact administrator for role assignment

**Problem**: Page loading slowly
**Solution**:
1. Check internet connection
2. Clear browser cache
3. Try refreshing the page

### Contact Information

For technical support or account issues:
- Contact your system administrator
- Provide your Unique ID when reporting issues
- Include specific error messages if available

## Security Guidelines

### Password Security
- Use strong passwords (minimum 8 characters)
- Include letters, numbers, and special characters
- Never share your password with others
- Change password regularly

### Session Security
- Always log out when finished
- Don't share your login credentials
- Avoid accessing the system on public computers
- Clear browser cache after use

### Data Protection
- Keep your personal information updated
- Report any suspicious activity immediately
- Don't share sensitive information via email
- Follow your organization's security policies
